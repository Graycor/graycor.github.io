QR code generator

Free, no sign-up and no tracking. Everything happens on your own device: nothing you type or upload ever leaves this page.

1. What should the code do?

Codes made here are static: they point straight at this address and can never be redirected later by anyone, including us.

Anyone who scans this code can join the network, so treat the printout like the password itself.

Scanning opens the phone's map app at this spot. To find a place's coordinates, right-click it in any map app and copy them.

Times are saved in the scanning phone's own time zone.
